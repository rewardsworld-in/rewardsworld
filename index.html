  • Growing private company

  • LLP
  • Bank

The tool is built around Indian law and Indian legal forms, from a start-up to a listed company, and for an LLP, a bank or an insurer. It asks for your legal form and your regulator, then shows the checks that apply to you. We do this work for organisations in India and for clients around the world. Anything not yet checked by a legal adviser is marked draft.

What you get

Three outputs from the work

Each piece of work ends in a page you can take into a meeting. Your data, its source and the name of who approved it sit on the page.

Board pack

A short board page that answers four questions in one place: what it costs this year, what it is meant to achieve, what it costs later, and what breaks in a bad year. Sources, assumptions and risks sit on the same page, with a version stamp and the name of who approved it.

Pay bands

A set of Fixed CTC bands by grade, with placement rules and a one-page view of where each person lands and what it costs to move them in.

Compliance check

A list of the Indian checks that apply to your legal form and your regulator, each with its source and date. Each line shows what needs attention, for review by your legal adviser. It is not a legal opinion.
