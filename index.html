The everyday problems we help with

  • Spreadsheets everywhere

    Pay data lives in many files and versions. We load it once, check it, and use it for every piece of work.

  • Typing the same data again

    Each service hands its data and results to the next, so nothing is keyed in twice.

  • Unclear approvals

    Every review is approved by a different named person, and every decision is recorded.

  • Board packs that take weeks

    Packs are built from the checked data, with sources, assumptions and risks on one page.

  • Keeping up with the rules

    Rules and limits carry their source and date. Anything not yet checked by a legal adviser is clearly marked draft.

  • Juniors who cannot explain the numbers

    Plain explanations on every screen, and a short check of understanding before a pack goes to the board.
